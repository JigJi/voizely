description: Future vision beyond MoM — role-based AI Copilot for BD, PM, Sales, HR
type: project
---

Planned direction: evolve from MoM tool to **AI Meeting Workspace** / **AI Copilot** per role.

**Concepts:**
- **Conversation Intelligence** (Sales): sentiment, talk ratio, objection detection, deal score, next steps
- **BD Head**: cross-meeting trends, pipeline from calls, team coaching alerts
- **PM**: requirement drift tracking, decision log, stakeholder risk flags
- **HR**: interview analysis, 1-on-1 feedback summaries

**Why:** MoM/document generation is commodity — every competitor does it. User wants to differentiate with deeper, role-specific intelligence.

**How to apply:** When building new features, prioritize analytics and cross-meeting insights over just single-meeting outputs. The moat is in connecting data across meetings, not just processing one.
